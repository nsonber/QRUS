Good to know

A few quick answers

Have another question? The organising team is happy to help.

Who can join the celebrations?

All 720 residents are warmly invited. Bring your household and come enjoy the festivities together.

What should I wear?

Wear whatever you feel comfortable celebrating in. Traditional and colourful outfits are always welcome.

Should I bring dandiya sticks?

If you have your own, bring them along. Availability of shared sticks will be confirmed by the organisers.

What time does Garba start?

Garba runs from 8:30 PM to 11:30 PM each night, Friday 16 through Monday 19 October.

Where is the venue?

We’ll celebrate on the ground floor at Q Residencies Premises. Use the map link in the venue section for directions.
